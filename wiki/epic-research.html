Honey, Inc. Epic Research: all 18 honeycomb upgrades

Epic research is bought with Honeycombs and kept forever — it is the only upgrade track in the game that survives a reincarnation reset.

Honey, Inc. epic research is a separate tree of 18 permanent upgrades, purchased with Honeycombs rather than cash. The headline fact is simple and it changes how you should play: epic upgrades persist through reincarnation. Your farm resets, your regular research tree resets, your buildings reset — the epic upgrades stay exactly where you left them.

That makes epic research the game's real long-term investment. Everything else you buy is rented for one run. These 18 are owned.

Why the persistence matters

Idle games live or die on how the reset feels, and Honey, Inc. answers that with two persistent things: Royal Jellies, which grant a capped earnings bonus, and epic research, which is not capped in the same way and covers far more of the farm. Because the Royal Jelly bonus tops out at +25%, the compounding part of your long-term power comes overwhelmingly from this tree.

The practical consequence is that Honeycombs spent on epic research can never be wasted by a badly timed reset. Honeycombs spent on boosts buy you speed right now; Honeycombs spent here buy you every run from now on. Both are legitimate, but if you are unsure, the permanent side is the safer default.

Where Honeycombs come from. Tapping wasps, opening crates, mail rewards, the daily login cycle, watching ads, and referrals. None of that income is lost at reset either — Honeycombs carry over with the epic upgrades they buy.

Below is the complete list, grouped by what part of the farm each one addresses. Exact values and Honeycomb costs are shown in the game's epic research screen, so this table describes what each upgrade addresses rather than reproducing numbers.

Three upgrades that reach into other systems

Most of the tree improves your farm in place. Three do something more interesting: they change how other systems pay out, which means their value depends on how you actually play.

Enhanced Wasp Drops is the most concrete. Wasps fly across the farm and give cash or Honeycombs when tapped, in rarity bands — common, uncommon and rare, plus an epic band on Elite wasps. Common wasps appear roughly every 30 to 90 seconds and Elites roughly every 8 to 14 minutes, so this upgrade rewards players who keep the app open and tap. If you play in short idle check-ins, it does less for you than it does for someone watching the farm.

Royal Jelly Amplification and Reincarnation Mastery both sit on the prestige loop. Because Royal Jellies grant +1.2% earnings each up to a +25% ceiling, the base prestige bonus is deliberately modest — these two are how the reset loop grows beyond that. They are worth most to players who intend to run many cycles rather than one very long farm.

Research Subsidies and Rapid Research Mode deserve a mention for the same structural reason. The regular tree — 13 tiers, 57 groups — is destroyed and rebuilt every cycle. Anything that makes rebuilding cheaper or faster is paid back once per reincarnation, forever.

How to spend Honeycombs

There is no single correct order, but there are honest principles.

  • Buy things you re-pay for. Upgrades touching the regular research tree and construction costs are re-earned every cycle, because you pay those costs again every cycle.
  • Match upgrades to your play pattern. Wasp drops reward active tapping. Nursery and storage-clock upgrades reward long absences. Buying the wrong half for how you actually use the game is the most common way to feel like epic research "did nothing".
  • Do not hoard. Honeycombs sitting in your balance produce nothing. Since the upgrades are permanent, spending early gives them more runs to compound over.
  • Broad multipliers before narrow ones. Something that multiplies swarm output touches the entire chain from bee to farm value; something that speeds one hive activation does not.

If you have not reincarnated yet, epic research can look like a side menu. It is the opposite — it is the part of the game that is still there tomorrow. New players should read how to play first, then beginner tips, and treat the first few Honeycombs they earn as seed money for this tree rather than as boost currency.
