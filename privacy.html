Honey Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Honey Inc. mobile game for iOS and Android and related online services (collectively, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the Services.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

Contact: honeyinc.app  ·  becomethehive@gmail.com

1 Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you, automatically through your use of the Services, and from third-party providers.

A. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use Honey Inc., we may collect:

  • Display name or profile name (for example, from Apple or Google sign-in, or a name you choose where available)
  • Email address (if provided by you or your sign-in provider; Apple may provide a private relay address)
  • Account and authentication information (managed through Firebase Authentication; we do not receive your Apple or Google password)
  • Guest play: if you use Guest mode, we create an anonymous account identifier without requiring an email or name
  • Referral or invite codes you voluntarily enter
  • Customer support communications (including information you send when contacting us for help)
  • Survey responses and feedback you choose to submit
  • User-generated content you submit through the Services (where such features are offered)
  • Information you provide when participating in promotions, contests, or events
  • Purchase-related information necessary to deliver in-app content (processed largely by app stores; see Section 4)

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Services, we may automatically collect:

Device Information

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • Device language and region settings
  • Mobile network information (where available)
  • IP address (received when your device connects to our servers or advertising partners)
  • Platform (iOS or Android)

Identifiers

  • Firebase user ID (including anonymous IDs for Guest accounts)
  • App instance and session identifiers used by our service providers
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android), where available and permitted by your device settings and applicable law

Gameplay Information

  • Game progress, buildings, bees, vehicles, research, and other in-game state
  • In-game currency and resources (including cash, farm value, Honeycombs, Royal Jelly, and related balances)
  • Achievements, prestige or reincarnation data, and progression history
  • Session duration and play time
  • In-app purchases and virtual item transactions (see Section 4)
  • Interaction with game features (including ads, referrals, mail, and rewards)
  • Preferences and settings (such as audio volume and performance options)

Storage on Your Device

  • We store game save data and certain preferences locally on your device (for example, using on-device storage such as AsyncStorage) so the game can run and remember your progress.

Cloud Data (Linked Accounts)

  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, we may store game saves, server-backed currency, referral data, transaction logs, and related account information in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Functions).

Usage Data

  • App version
  • Login and activity timestamps (such as last login or last active time)
  • Server and function logs related to account sync, currency validation, referrals, and ad rewards
  • Diagnostic and performance information where collected by our service providers

Advertising Data

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and completions
  • Rewarded ad watch duration and reward type (logged on our servers when you complete rewarded ads)
  • Campaign or attribution data from advertising partners, where applicable

C. Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple): user identifier and, if you choose to share them, email and name
  • Google (Sign in with Google): user identifier, email, display name, and profile photo URL if available
  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, and cloud function services used to operate accounts and game data
  • Google AdMob: advertising delivery and measurement
  • Mobile app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play): purchase and subscription status needed to verify transactions
  • Analytics and diagnostics providers we or our partners use to understand usage and improve stability
  • Payment processors and platforms that handle in-app purchases on our behalf
  • Social media or authentication platforms, if you choose to connect them where such options are offered

We do not control how third parties collect or use their own information. Their practices are governed by their privacy policies.

Provide and Operate the Services

  • Create and maintain accounts (including Guest and linked Apple/Google accounts)

  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests

Improve the Services

  • Analyze gameplay trends and feature usage
  • Fix bugs, crashes, and technical issues
  • Improve game balance, performance, and features
  • Develop new content and events

Personalize User Experience

  • Customize gameplay and in-game experiences where appropriate
  • Deliver content and offers relevant to your use of the Services

Marketing and Communications

  • Send service-related notifications (including account, security, or policy updates)
  • Inform users about updates, events, and new features
  • Send promotional communications where permitted by law and your preferences

Advertising

  • Display advertisements through partners such as Google AdMob
  • Measure ad performance and grant in-game rewards for completed ads where offered

Security and Compliance

  • Prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect users and the Services
  • Comply with legal obligations

  • Performance of a contract

4 In-App Purchases and Payment Information

Honey Inc. may offer in-app purchases and virtual currency through third-party platforms, including:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play Store
  • Other authorized payment providers we may use in the future

We do not generally receive or store full payment card or bank account details. Payment processing is handled by the applicable platform or payment processor according to their privacy policies.

We may receive transaction-related information (such as purchase identifiers, product IDs, purchase time, and subscription status) necessary to verify purchases, prevent fraud, and provide purchased or unlocked content.

5 Advertising

Honey Inc. may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob (rewarded and interstitial formats).

Advertising partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Usage and ad interaction information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

This information may be used to:

  • Deliver and measure advertisements
  • Prevent advertising fraud
  • Provide personalized advertising where permitted by law and your device settings

On iOS, tracking for personalized ads may be limited unless you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework where we implement it. You may also limit personalized advertising through your device settings (for example, “Limit Ad Tracking” on iOS or ad personalization settings on Android).

When you complete rewarded advertisements, we may log reward details and watch duration on our servers to grant in-game benefits and maintain fair use limits.

6 Analytics

We use analytics and operational data to understand how players use the Services and to keep them running reliably. This may include:

  • Device and app information
  • Session and gameplay behavior
  • Feature usage and progression metrics
  • Crash, error, and performance data from our service providers

Primary systems involved in understanding usage today include our own server logs (Firebase Cloud Functions and Firestore), advertising partner reporting (AdMob), and analytics or diagnostics tools we enable from time to time. Analytics data helps us improve performance, functionality, security, and player experience.

7 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Honey Inc. is primarily a mobile application. We and our partners may use technologies such as:

  • Software development kits (SDKs)
  • Local and on-device storage
  • Pixels or similar technologies in linked web pages (if any)
  • Cookies, where you access web-based portions of the Services

These technologies help us:

  • Operate the Services and maintain sessions
  • Remember preferences and game progress
  • Analyze usage and diagnose problems
  • Improve functionality
  • Measure advertising performance

You may manage certain tracking or advertising preferences through your device or account settings and, where applicable, in-app choices.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Services
  • Fulfill the purposes described in this Policy
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

Retention periods vary by data type. For example, account and gameplay data may be kept while your account is active; certain transaction or anti-fraud records may be retained longer where required by law or legitimate business need.
