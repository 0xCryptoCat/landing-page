Honey, Inc. Wiki

Every mechanic in the game, written out properly: how bees and hives actually work, what each building multiplies, all ten honey types, and when reincarnation is worth it.

Honey, Inc. is a free idle bee farm game for iOS and Android, also playable inside Telegram with no download. You spawn bees, they produce honey, honey gets stored and shipped for cash, and cash buys the upgrades that make the next loop faster. It keeps running while you are away.

If you are new, start with How to Play and then Beginner Tips. If you are deep in and wondering whether to reset, go to Reincarnation.
